Automation

What n8n actually does

n8n is a workflow tool that connects apps and APIs — a form submission, a webhook, an email — into one automated sequence. Instead of someone copying a lead from an inbox into a spreadsheet, n8n moves it, checks it, and updates the CRM on its own.

Amazon

FBA vs Wholesale vs Private Label

FBA is a fulfilment method, not a business model — you can run wholesale or private label through it. Wholesale means reselling existing branded products; Private Label means selling under your own brand on a manufactured product. Associates is a separate, commission-based referral model.

Shopify

Five things that make a store convert

Clear navigation, fast page load, visible trust signals (reviews, guarantees), a checkout under three steps, and a mobile-first layout. Most conversion problems trace back to one of these five, not to traffic volume.

AI & CRM

How AI lead qualification works

A lead comes in through a form or chat, an AI step reads it and scores intent, budget and priority against rules you set, then routes it — a hot lead pings a human immediately, a cold one goes into a nurture sequence.

Growth

SEO vs PPC — when to use which

PPC buys attention now — useful when you need leads this month. SEO builds a channel that keeps producing traffic without ongoing spend, but takes months to show results. Most growing businesses eventually run both, not one instead of the other.

Automation

Why automate a repetitive task once

A task done manually every day costs time every day, indefinitely. The same task automated once costs a build fee, then close to nothing to keep running. The break-even point is usually faster than it looks once you count the hours honestly.
