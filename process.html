AI Systems & n8n

A practical automation lifecycle.

Every system we build, regardless of which service it sits under, runs through the same six stages — capture, normalize, decide, act, verify, report.

01

Capture

Forms, webhooks, email, schedules and inbound messages.

02

Normalize

Validate, clean, map, deduplicate and structure data.

03

Decide

Rules, AI classification, priority and routing.

04

Act

CRM write, email, notification, API action, update.

05

Verify

Fallbacks, retries, status checks and human escalation.

06

Report

Logs, summaries, hand-offs, KPI-ready records.

Under the Hood

What powers each stage

n8n Triggers

Nodes, branching, expressions, data mapping, scheduled workflows, reusable subflows.

AI Agents

Prompt design, structured outputs, classification, summarization, tool use, escalation.

Integration

REST / HTTP, webhooks, JSON payloads, authentication patterns, third-party SaaS.

CRM / Intake

Lead capture, customer intake, qualification, CRM updates, follow-up, alerts.

Reliability

Validation, retries, failure routes, manual-review gates, status logging, handover.

Business Ops

Gmail, Google Sheets, Telegram, forms, notifications, support workflows.
