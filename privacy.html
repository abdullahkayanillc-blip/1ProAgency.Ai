Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit 1PROAGENCY.AI or contact us.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

1PROAGENCY.AI is operated by Abdullah Kayani. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to 1PROAGENCY.AI. For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the Contact page.

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the site, you may submit your name, email address, country, selected service or bundle, project message, budget, timeline, newsletter details, or messages entered in the chat widget. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, sensitive personal information, or information you are not authorized to share.

How submissions are sent

The project contact form sends the fields you submit to our configured n8n intake service. If that request fails, the form attempts delivery through Formspree. Newsletter signups and messages sent through the chat widget are sent to the n8n intake service; those features do not use the Formspree fallback. These providers process submitted information to transmit or handle your request, subject to their own terms and privacy notices. A successful submission response does not establish what downstream actions a provider or connected workflow completed.

We use the information you provide to respond to project enquiries, handle requests, provide a requested newsletter, maintain the site, and protect against misuse. We do not sell personal information through this website.

Technical information and third parties

Our hosting and security providers may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser details, and request time, to deliver and protect the site. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google to retrieve those fonts. Those providers have their own privacy practices.

This site does not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. Client login is not currently configured, and the dashboard does not retrieve private service records. If an authentication service is enabled later, that service may process sign-in information under its own privacy notice, and this policy will be updated as appropriate.

Retention and security

We and the providers handling submissions retain information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide a requested service, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or security obligations. Actual retention can depend on the provider and any separate agreement. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or limits on the use of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us through the Contact page to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where the law permits or requires it. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the instructions included in those messages.

Children and policy updates

This site is intended for business enquiries and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. We may update this policy as the site or its data handling changes; the date above indicates the latest revision.
